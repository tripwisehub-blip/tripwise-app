# Tripwise — cheap flights, hot deals and travel guides

Tripwise is a free travel assistant that helps you find good-value trips from anywhere in the world.

- **Telegram bot:** [@tripwise_hub_bot](https://t.me/tripwise_hub_bot) — tell it where and when you want to go (text or voice, 20+ languages) and get tips on areas, tours, transfers, car rental and eSIMs.
- **Deals channel:** [@tripwise_deals](https://t.me/tripwise_deals) — every day the 3 cheapest return flights from a European city, hotel deals and a travel guide of the day.
- **Web app:** [tripwisehub-blip.github.io/tripwise-app](https://tripwisehub-blip.github.io/tripwise-app/) — flight search, the budget wheel (3 trips that fit your budget: cheap, middle, top) and price-drop alerts.
- **Travel guides:** [Tripwise Guides](https://tripwisehub-blip.github.io/tripwise-app/blog/) — practical city guides by country: Paris, London, Rome, Barcelona, Lisbon, Vienna, Budapest, Prague, Kraków, Istanbul, Antalya, Dubai, Hurghada, Bangkok, Bali, Tenerife, Madeira, the Baltics and more.

Some links are affiliate links; prices for travellers stay the same.
