Helsinki Airport to the city centre: train, bus and taxi

How to get from Helsinki-Vantaa Airport to Helsinki centre: the airport train, buses, taxis and transfers, with ticket tips.

Updated October 2026 · Prices and schedules change – always check current details before booking.

Helsinki-Vantaa Airport (HEL) is about 20 km north of the city centre. Public transport is easy, frequent and reasonably priced.

The airport train (I and P lines)

The Ring Rail Line stops in a station directly under the airport. Both the I and P trains go to Helsinki Central Station – they just travel around the loop in opposite directions, and the trip takes about 30 minutes. Trains run every 10 minutes or so for most of the day.

Tickets

  • The airport is in HSL zone C, so you need an ABC ticket to reach the centre.
  • Buy it in the HSL app, from ticket machines at the station, or pay with a contactless bank card at the readers on the platform.
  • If you will use public transport a lot, a day ticket for zones ABC can be better value.

Buses

Bus 600 runs from the airport to the Railway Square in the centre and takes longer than the train, but it is useful for stops along the way and at night.

Taxi and ride-hailing

Taxis are available outside arrivals; the ride to the centre takes about 25–35 minutes. Finnish taxis are not cheap, so check fixed-price options in the taxi apps or at the taxi rank.

Pre-booked transfer

With children, lots of luggage or a late flight, a pre-booked transfer with a fixed price is the least stressful choice.
