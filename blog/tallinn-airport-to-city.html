Tallinn Airport to the city centre: tram, bus, taxi and transfer

How to get from Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport to the Old Town and the harbour – the cheapest and fastest options explained.

Updated October 2026 · Prices and schedules change – always check current details before booking.

Tallinn Airport (Lennart Meri, TLL) is one of the closest airports to a capital city centre in Europe – only about 4 km from the Old Town. Getting into town is quick and cheap.

Tram 4 – the easiest option

Tram line 4 stops right next to the terminal and goes to the city centre (Viru Keskus and Hobujaama stops are next to the Old Town) in about 15–20 minutes. Trams run frequently from early morning until around midnight.

Bus 2

City bus 2 also connects the airport with the centre and continues to the passenger port (A-terminal). It is handy if you are going straight to the Helsinki ferry – see our Tallinn–Helsinki guide.

Tickets

  • The simplest way to pay is to tap a contactless bank card or phone on the validator when you board – it buys a time ticket automatically.
  • You can also buy tickets in the Pilet.ee app or a Ühiskaart travel card.
  • Registered Tallinn residents travel free, but visitors must pay.

Taxi and ride-hailing

Taxis wait outside arrivals, and ride-hailing apps such as Bolt (an Estonian company) work well. The ride to the Old Town takes about 10–15 minutes. Check the price on the sticker in the taxi window before you get in.

Pre-booked transfer

For families, late-night arrivals or groups with luggage, a pre-booked transfer with a fixed price and a meeting sign is the most relaxed option.

Renting a car

Rental desks are in the arrivals hall. A car is not needed in the city, but it is great for day trips to Lahemaa National Park, Pärnu or Tartu. Compare car rental prices in advance – it is usually cheaper than booking on the spot.
