New Year's Eve in Europe on a budget: 8 cities and how to book without overpaying

Where to celebrate New Year's Eve in Europe without spending a fortune: 8 good-value cities, when to book flights and hotels, what's free on the night and how to get home after midnight.

Updated October 2026. Prices and schedules change, so check current details before booking.

New Year's Eve is one of the most expensive nights of the year to travel: hotels add minimum stays, flights on 30–31 December sell out and taxis disappear after midnight. But you can still have a great night in a European city without paying peak-of-peak prices – if you choose the right place and book in the right order.

When to book

  • Flights first, now. Fares for 29–31 December rise fast in late November and December. Flying on 29 or 30 December and back on 2 January is usually cheaper than 31 December / 1 January. Compare flights for flexible dates.
  • Hotels with free cancellation. Many places require 2–3 nights over New Year. Book a room you can cancel for free, then keep checking – prices sometimes drop in the last two weeks.
  • Avoid the main square. Rooms right next to the fireworks are the most expensive and the noisiest. Stay one or two tram stops away.

8 good-value cities for New Year's Eve

1. Prague

Old Town Square and Wenceslas Square fill with people, and the views from Letná Park and the bridges are free. Beer and food stay cheaper than in Western Europe. Book early – Prague is one of the most popular New Year destinations. Hotels in Prague · Prague on a budget

2. Budapest

Crowds gather along the Danube and around the Basilica; the bridges and the Buda side give great views. On 1 January, recover in a thermal bath. Hotels in Budapest · Budapest on a budget

3. Kraków

The Main Market Square hosts a big free open-air party, and the compact Old Town means you can walk home. Prices are among the lowest of the big European party cities. Hotels in Kraków · A weekend in Kraków

4. Lisbon

The mildest weather on this list: the riverside square Praça do Comércio is the centre of the celebrations, and the viewpoints (miradouros) are free. Good if you want New Year without snow. Hotels in Lisbon

5. Vienna

The Silvesterpfad – a New Year's trail of stages and stalls through the old town – is free to walk. On 1 January you can catch the famous New Year's Concert on big screens. Vienna isn't cheap, but trams and the metro run all night. Hotels in Vienna · Vienna in 2–3 days

6. Tallinn

A fairy-tale medieval Old Town, often with snow, and a free concert and fireworks in Freedom Square. Short flights and ferries make it an easy add-on to Helsinki. Hotels in Tallinn · Tallinn on a budget

7. Riga

Fireworks over the Daugava river, best seen from the embankment near the Old Town. Riga is usually cheaper than Tallinn for hotels over New Year. Hotels in Riga

8. Berlin

Berlin is huge, so you'll find everything from clubs to private fireworks in every neighbourhood. Hostels and budget hotels are plentiful, and public transport runs through the night. Hotels in Berlin

What's free on the night

  • Fireworks and the countdown in main squares are free almost everywhere. Some cities fence off the central zone or limit entry when it's full – arrive by 22:00 or watch from a viewpoint or bridge.
  • Official programmes change every year (some cities replace fireworks with light or drone shows), so check the city's official website a week before.
  • Museums are mostly closed on 1 January; plan a walk, a thermal bath or a guided tour instead. For city sights with fixed tickets, see attraction tickets.

Getting there and back safely

  • Airport transfer. Taxis are scarce and expensive on 31 December and 1 January. A pre-booked transfer with a fixed price saves stress.
  • After midnight. Check whether the metro or trams run all night – in many cities they do on New Year's Eve. Otherwise stay within walking distance.
  • Mobile data. Networks are overloaded at midnight, but you'll need maps and tickets the rest of the trip. A travel eSIM is cheaper than roaming outside the EU.
  • Pickpockets. Crowded squares at midnight are prime time. Keep your phone and wallet in a front pocket.

Quick budget plan

  1. Pick 2–3 cities from the list and compare fares for 29 Dec – 2 Jan.
  2. Book the cheapest flight, then a hotel with free cancellation one or two stops from the centre.
  3. Pre-book the airport transfer and download offline maps.
  4. Keep checking the hotel price until about a week before.
