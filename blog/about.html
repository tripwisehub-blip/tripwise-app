About Tripwise

Tripwise helps travellers plan good-value trips. We publish practical travel guides and run a free travel assistant in Telegram, @tripwise_hub_bot, that answers questions in any language – including voice messages – and suggests areas to stay, tours, transport and money-saving tips.

The Tripwise web app lets you search flights in many currencies, find weekend destinations that fit your budget with the budget wheel, and get price-drop alerts for a chosen route.

Our principles

  • Useful first: every guide should help you make a real decision.
  • No invented prices or fake reviews – we tell you to check current prices with the provider.
  • Clear disclosure of affiliate links.
