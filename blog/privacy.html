Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Website and web app

These guides do not use advertising cookies or user accounts. The web app stores your settings (language, currency, departure city) only in your own browser. Partner websites you open through our links have their own privacy policies and may use cookies to track bookings.

Telegram bot

When you message @tripwise_hub_bot, we process your messages to answer them, using an AI service. We store your Telegram chat ID and, if you turn on a price alert, the route and dates you chose, so that we can send you updates. Send /stopalerts to remove your alerts. We do not sell personal data.
