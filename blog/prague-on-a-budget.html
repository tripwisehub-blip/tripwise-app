Prague on a budget: how to enjoy the city without tourist prices

Practical tips for Prague: the best areas, sights worth paying for, and how to avoid tourist traps.

Updated October 2026 · Prices and schedules change – always check current details before booking.

Prague is one of Europe's most beautiful cities and still good value if you avoid the busiest tourist spots for eating and exchanging money.

The classic route

Start at Prague Castle and St Vitus Cathedral early in the morning, walk down through Malá Strana to Charles Bridge, and continue to the Old Town Square and the Astronomical Clock. The castle grounds are free; the main buildings need a ticket.

Beyond the centre

  • Vyšehrad – a quiet fortress with river views and a historic cemetery.
  • Letná Park – a beer garden with one of the best views of the bridges.
  • Vinohrady and Žižkov – local cafés and pubs with lower prices.

Money tips

  • Pay by card or withdraw from bank ATMs; avoid exchange offices with "0% commission" signs and bad rates.
  • Lunch menus ("polední menu") on weekdays are the cheapest way to eat well.
  • A public transport ticket covers trams, metro and the funicular to Petřín Hill.

Getting there

Bus 59 and the Airport Express connect Václav Havel Airport with the metro and the main station. Prefer a direct ride? Book a private transfer. Use a flight comparison to find the cheapest days to fly.
