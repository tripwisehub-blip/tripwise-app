Istanbul in four days: two continents, one itinerary

A practical Istanbul itinerary with the main sights, ferries, food tips and where to stay.

Istanbul straddles Europe and Asia across the Bosphorus. It's huge, so a plan by area saves a lot of time.

Day 1: Sultanahmet

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern and the Hippodrome are all within walking distance. Dress modestly for mosques; women need a headscarf. Visit early, and avoid prayer times.

Day 2: Topkapı and the bazaars

Topkapı Palace deserves half a day. Then walk to the Grand Bazaar and the Spice Bazaar. Haggling is normal, so start lower and stay friendly.

Day 3: Beyoğlu and Galata

Walk down İstiklal Avenue to the Galata Tower and Karaköy's cafés. In the evening, take a ferry across the Bosphorus – one of the best-value views in the city.

Day 4: The Asian side

Ferry to Kadıköy for its market, street food and relaxed atmosphere, and walk along the Moda seafront.

Practical tips

  • Get an Istanbulkart for trams, metro, buses and ferries.
  • Istanbul Airport is far from the centre (about an hour); the metro or a pre-booked transfer is easier than haggling with taxis.
  • Book a Bosphorus cruise or skip-the-line tickets for the busiest sights.
  • An eSIM saves money if you are coming from outside Türkiye.
