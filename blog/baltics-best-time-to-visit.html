Best time to visit the Baltics: a month-by-month guide

When to visit Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania: white nights, Christmas markets, snowy winters and the best months for good prices.

Updated October 2026 · Prices and schedules change – always check current details before booking.

Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania have four very different seasons. The best time depends on what you want – long summer days, festivals, or quiet, cheap city breaks.

Summer (June–August)

The most popular season. Days are very long – around midsummer it barely gets dark – and the weather is usually 18–25 °C. Midsummer (23–24 June: Jaanipäev, Līgo and Joninės) is the biggest holiday of the year, with bonfires and festivals in the countryside. Beaches in Pärnu, Jūrmala and Palanga are busy. Book flights and hotels early.

Autumn (September–October)

September is often still mild, and prices drop after the school holidays. October brings autumn colours in the national parks and quiet old towns. A great time for city breaks.

Winter (November–March)

Short days and frost, but beautiful: Christmas markets in Tallinn, Riga and Vilnius from late November, snow-covered old towns and cosy cafés. January to March is usually the cheapest time for flights and hotels. Bring warm clothes and good shoes.

Spring (April–May)

Days get longer quickly, parks turn green in May, and the summer crowds have not arrived yet. Good value and good weather for walking.

Quick summary

  • Best weather: June–August.
  • Best value: January–March and October–November.
  • Christmas atmosphere: late November–December.
  • Balanced choice: May and September.
