Lapland in winter: how to plan a Rovaniemi trip without overpaying

Northern lights, husky safaris and Santa Claus Village: timing, budget and booking tips for Finnish Lapland.

Updated October 2026 · Prices and schedules change – always check current details before booking.

Rovaniemi, on the Arctic Circle, is the gateway to Finnish Lapland. Between December and March it offers snow, husky and reindeer safaris and – with luck – the northern lights. It is popular, so planning ahead makes a big difference to the price.

When to go

  • Late November – December: Christmas atmosphere, but only a few hours of daylight and the highest prices around the holidays.
  • January – February: deep snow and cold (often −15 to −30 °C), fewer families, lower prices. Good aurora chances on clear nights.
  • March: longer days, sunshine on snow, still good aurora odds – great value.

Getting there

Rovaniemi has an airport with direct winter flights from many European cities, plus domestic flights from Helsinki. The overnight Santa Claus Express train from Helsinki is a scenic alternative. Flight prices rise sharply for Christmas dates – compare dates a few months ahead.

What to do

  • Santa Claus Village – free to enter, right on the Arctic Circle line.
  • Husky and reindeer safaris – book early for December.
  • Northern lights tours that drive away from city lights when the sky is clear.
  • Arktikum museum for Arctic science and Sámi culture.

Popular activities and safaris sell out in high season. If you want to chase the aurora yourself, a rental car with winter tyres gives you freedom – drive carefully and watch for reindeer on the road.

Packing and budget tips

  • Layer: thermal base layer, fleece, insulated jacket, warm boots and mittens. Many safaris include thermal overalls.
  • Self-catering apartments and cabins cut food costs considerably.
  • Stay a few kilometres outside the centre for darker skies and lower rates.
