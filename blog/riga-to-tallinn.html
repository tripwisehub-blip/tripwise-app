Riga to Tallinn: bus, train, car or flight

How to travel between Riga and Tallinn: comfortable coaches, the train with a change in Valga, driving the Via Baltica, and short flights.

Updated October 2026 · Prices and schedules change – always check current details before booking.

Riga and Tallinn are about 310 km apart, and many travellers visit both capitals on one trip. Here are the options, from cheapest to most flexible.

Bus – the most popular option

Comfortable coaches (Lux Express, FlixBus and others) run many times a day between the two bus stations and take about 4–4.5 hours. Most have Wi-Fi, power sockets and screens. Book online for the best fares; early-morning and late-evening departures are often cheapest.

Train with a change in Valga

There is no direct fast train yet (the new Rail Baltica line is still being built), but you can travel by rail: a Latvian train from Riga to Valga, then an Estonian Elron train from Valga to Tallinn. It takes longer than the bus, but it is a scenic, relaxed way to travel. Check the timetables of both operators so the connection works.

By car

The Via Baltica road (A1 in Latvia, route 4 in Estonia) takes about 4 hours. A car lets you stop in Pärnu, Estonia's summer capital, or in the Gauja National Park near Sigulda. If you rent a car in one country and return it in the other, check the one-way fee – compare rental offers before booking.

Flight

The flight is about 50 minutes and can be useful for connections, but it is rarely faster door to door than the bus. Compare flight prices anyway – sometimes there are good deals.

Which should you choose?

  • Lowest price and simplest: bus.
  • Most scenic and relaxed: train via Valga.
  • Most flexible, with stops on the way: car.
