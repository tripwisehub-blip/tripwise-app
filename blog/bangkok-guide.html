Bangkok first trip: temples, food and getting around

Where to stay in Bangkok, how to use the river boats and Skytrain, and how to avoid common scams.

Updated October 2026 · Prices and schedules change – always check current details before booking.

Bangkok is intense, delicious and surprisingly affordable. A bit of planning helps you skip the traffic and the touts.

Where to stay

  • Sukhumvit – on the BTS Skytrain, with lots of hotels and restaurants.
  • Riverside – close to the boats and the old city sights.
  • Silom – business district with night markets and good transport links.

Top sights

  • The Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew – dress code applies (covered shoulders and knees).
  • Wat Pho with the reclining Buddha, and Wat Arun across the river.
  • Chatuchak Weekend Market and the canals (khlongs) of Thonburi.

Getting around

Use the BTS Skytrain and MRT metro to beat traffic, and the Chao Phraya Express boats for riverside sights. From Suvarnabhumi Airport, the Airport Rail Link reaches the city in about 30 minutes; a private transfer is simpler late at night.

Avoid common scams

  • If someone says a sight is "closed today" and offers a tuk-tuk tour, politely decline.
  • Use metered taxis or ride-hailing apps; agree on the price before a tuk-tuk ride.

Plan ahead

Day trips to Ayutthaya, the floating markets or a Thai cooking class can be booked online. Stay connected with an eSIM for Thailand, and compare flights to Bangkok across airlines.
